Texture Prep Kit

Turn a real top-down photo of laid stone into a visualizer texture with an AI image editor, without letting the AI change the product. Prepare the photo, copy the prompt, then check the AI result here before uploading it.

1Before the AI

  1. Shoot as in the Stone Texture Standard: laid sample, straight down, overcast, tapes on two edges.
  2. Crop it yourself to an exact square between two tape marks (for example 0–150 cm) in any photo editor. AI models are not precise with scale, so this crop is what fixes the real size. Note the size.
  3. Use the best AI editor you have. Gemini (Nano Banana Pro) keeps the original pixels most faithfully and outputs up to 4K; ChatGPT image editing also works. Start a new chat for every product and attach the cropped photo.
